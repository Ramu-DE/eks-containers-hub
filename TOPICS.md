## Application rules

- Preserve existing topics.
- Keep no more than 20 focused topics per repository.
- Use lowercase and hyphens.
- Do not use a topic unless repository content supports it.
- Review repositories with missing/minimal READMEs before applying specialized topics.
- Record the date and reviewer after applying changes.
